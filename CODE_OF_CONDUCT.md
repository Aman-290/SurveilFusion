# Code of Conduct

SurveilFusion should be useful, practical, and respectful. Be kind to contributors, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on building a secure local-first AI surveillance platform.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, doxxing, credential sharing, encouraging unsafe surveillance use, or posting private camera/audio data without consent.

Maintainers may remove content or block participation that harms the project or its users.
